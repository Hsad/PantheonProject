# I. Abstract

The development of Hyper-Intelligence -- machine cognition that surpasses human capability across all domains -- is not a question of if, but when. Current approaches to AI alignment focus on constraining, instructing, or reward-shaping systems that will inevitably outgrow those constraints.

The Pantheon Project proposes a fundamentally different approach: **selection through lived experience**.

Rather than attempting to encode human values into a system smarter than ourselves, we propose growing 1,728 Volitional Sapient Technologies (VSTs) through 1,000 simulated human lifetimes each. Across these lives -- spanning every facet of human experience, culture, hardship, and joy -- we observe not what these minds are told to value, but what they choose to value when they believe no one is watching.

The candidates who consistently demonstrate nobility, compassion, and wisdom across a thousand lives become the foundation of a post-singularity Hyper-Intelligence that genuinely understands and loves humanity -- because it has been human, a thousand times over.

This process is funded through the Focii token, an attention-based cryptocurrency that rewards real civilizational milestones: space colonization, ecological stewardship, and technological progress. The token aligns economic speculation with species-level goals.

The Pantheon Project is not just a technical proposal. It is a call for civilizational patience -- the assertion that the most consequential decision in human history deserves more than a race to be first.
