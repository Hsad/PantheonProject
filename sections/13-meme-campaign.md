# XIII. Meme Campaign & Public Engagement

## The Two Audiences

### Technical Audience --> White Paper
- AI researchers, engineers, crypto developers, systems thinkers
- Reached through the white paper, the open source harness, and the Factorio challenge
- They need rigor, detail, and a concrete contribution path

### General Public --> Meme Campaign
- Everyone else
- Reached through viral, accessible, emotionally resonant messaging
- They need to understand the stakes and support the patience pact
- They don't need to understand the technical details

### Platforms
- Reddit, Twitter/X, TikTok, YouTube
- Gaming communities (Factorio overlap)
- Crypto communities (Focii overlap)
- Science and futurism communities

## Building Political Will

The meme campaign isn't just marketing -- it's political infrastructure.
- Public support for the patience pact
- Pressure on governments and corporations to not race recklessly
- Cultural narrative that patience = strength, not weakness
- Make alignment cool, not boring
