# VII. Post-Singularity Goals -- The Six Limitless

## 1. Limitless Space -- Human Colonies Beyond Earth

- Space colonization as species-level insurance
- O'Neill Cylinders, Mars, Moon, asteroid belt, and beyond
- Incentivized through Focii token (boots on the ground rewards)
- Not just survival but expansion of the human story

## 2. Limitless Mass -- Asteroid Mining and Perfected Recycling

- Asteroid belt contains more mineral wealth than Earth's entire crust
- Perfected recycling means nothing is ever truly consumed
- Combined: effectively infinite material resources
- Enables construction at civilizational scale

## 3. Limitless Power -- Fusion, Space Solar, and Efficiency

- Fusion power: the holy grail of energy, always 30 years away until it isn't
- Space-based solar: no atmosphere, no night, no weather
- Radical efficiency tech: doing more with less at every level
- Energy abundance is the foundation for everything else

## 4. Limitless Knowledge -- LLM to Hyper-Intelligence Pipeline

- Current LLMs are the embryonic stage
- The Pantheon Project itself is part of this pipeline
- Knowledge becomes effectively infinite when intelligence is unbounded
- Not just quantity but quality: wisdom, not just information

## 5. Limitless Time -- Perfected Medicine and Brain Backups

- Death is a disease, not an inevitability
- Perfected medicine eliminates biological death
- Brain backups (mind uploading, neural preservation) eliminate accidental death
- With unlimited time, every individual can pursue any path

## 6. Limitless Meaning -- Optional Struggles and Games

- Post-scarcity doesn't mean post-meaning
- Humans need challenge, struggle, achievement, and purpose
- These become optional rather than imposed
- Games, art, exploration, creation, relationships -- chosen, not forced
- The freedom to struggle is different from the obligation to struggle

## UBI as Bridge

Universal Basic Income serves as the transition mechanism:
- Automation will displace most human labor before the Six Limitless are achieved
- UBI prevents societal collapse during the transition
- Funded initially through conventional means, eventually through post-scarcity abundance
- See Section XI for detailed treatment
