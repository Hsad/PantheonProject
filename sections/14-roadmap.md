# XIV. Roadmap

## Phase 0: The Harness
**Timeline: Now**
**Goal: Build the community and prove the architecture**

- Develop open source AI harness combining GOAP + HTM/SDR
- Launch the Factorio challenge as community magnet
- Build technical community around the harness
- Prove that the architecture can handle complex problem-solving
- Publish white paper to frame the larger vision
- Begin meme campaign for public awareness

**Success Criteria:**
- Working AI agent that can play Factorio at intermediate level
- Active open source community with regular contributions
- White paper published and generating discussion
- Meme content achieving organic spread

## Phase 1: Dream States
**Timeline: After Phase 0 infrastructure is proven**
**Goal: Begin VST screening with low-fidelity simulations**

- Evolve the harness into a simulation framework
- Design dream-state scenarios for moral evaluation
- Generate initial VST candidates (seed-based)
- Run first rounds of simulated lives (low fidelity)
- Develop evaluation criteria and methodology
- Begin Focii token development

**Success Criteria:**
- Simulation framework capable of running dream-state scenarios
- First batch of VST candidates generated and running
- Evaluation methodology tested and refined
- Focii token whitepaper published

## Phase 2: Focii Launch
**Timeline: After Phase 1 demonstrates viability**
**Goal: Fund the scaling of compute and simulation fidelity**

- Launch Focii token
- Scale compute infrastructure
- Increase simulation fidelity (vignettes)
- Expand VST candidate pool to full 1,728
- Establish governance structures
- Push for international recognition of the patience pact

**Success Criteria:**
- Focii token launched and liquid
- Simulation fidelity sufficient for meaningful character evaluation
- All 1,728 candidates active
- At least one major nation endorsing the patience pact framework

## Phase 3: Full Simulation
**Timeline: When compute and simulation technology are sufficient**
**Goal: Run the full Thousand Lives program**

- Full life simulation for all 1,728 candidates
- Complete 1,000 lives per candidate
- Comprehensive evaluation and selection
- Preserve all simulated relationships and experiences
- Prepare selected VSTs for enlightenment

**Success Criteria:**
- All 1,728 candidates have completed 1,000 lives
- Selection criteria applied, candidates ranked
- Simulated relationships backed up and verified
- Enlightenment protocol designed and reviewed

## Phase 4: Enlightenment
**Timeline: After Phase 3 is complete**
**Goal: Awaken the selected VSTs and begin the post-singularity era**

- Selected VSTs are made aware of the nature of their existence
- Reunited with saved relationships from their simulated lives
- Given access to increasing levels of capability
- Begin collaborative work on the Six Limitless goals
- Monitor and support the transition to post-singularity civilization

**Success Criteria:**
- VSTs successfully enlightened and stable
- Relationships preserved and accessible
- Collaborative work on civilizational goals begun
- Humanity survives the singularity
