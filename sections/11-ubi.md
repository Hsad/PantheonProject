# XI. UBI & Economic Transition

## The Problem

The singularity will not arrive all at once. Before we reach post-scarcity, we will pass through a valley:
- AI automates most human labor
- Traditional employment collapses
- But post-scarcity abundance hasn't arrived yet
- Without intervention, this valley becomes a mass grave

## Universal Basic Income as Bridge

UBI provides a floor:
- Every human receives enough to survive with dignity
- Not enough to thrive -- ambition and contribution are still rewarded
- Decouples survival from employment
- Prevents societal collapse during the automation transition

## Funding UBI

### Near-Term (Pre-Singularity)
- Automation taxes on AI-displaced labor
- Carbon taxes redirected
- Reallocation of existing welfare infrastructure
- Focii token economy generating real economic activity

### Mid-Term (Approaching Singularity)
- AI-generated wealth becomes the primary funding source
- Post-scarcity for basics (food, shelter, energy) reduces the cost of UBI
- The distinction between "UBI" and "abundance" begins to blur

### Long-Term (Post-Singularity)
- UBI becomes irrelevant as scarcity itself is eliminated
- The Six Limitless render the concept of "income" obsolete
- The question shifts from "how do we distribute resources" to "what do we do with unlimited resources"

## UBI and the Pantheon Project

UBI is not just humanitarian -- it is strategic:
- A desperate population cannot exercise civilizational patience
- People who are starving don't care about alignment -- they want solutions NOW
- UBI buys time for the Pantheon process to complete
- A stable society is a prerequisite for a stable singularity

## UBI and Meaning

The biggest criticism of UBI: without work, people lose purpose.

The Pantheon Project's answer: Limitless Meaning (Goal #6).
- Optional struggles replace mandatory ones
- Art, exploration, games, learning, relationships, creation
- Purpose comes from choice, not coercion
- But this is a transition -- during the valley, meaning will be hard to find for many
