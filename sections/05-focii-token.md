# V. The Focii Token

## Purpose

The Pantheon Project requires civilizational-scale compute sustained over a long period. Traditional funding models (government grants, corporate investment, donations) are insufficient for the scale and duration required. The Focii token solves this by aligning financial speculation with civilizational progress.

## Token Economics

### Monthly Tranche Model

A fixed quantity of Focii is minted each month in a transparent, predictable tranche. No surprise minting, no inflationary shocks. Each tranche is split across four non-overlapping distribution pools:

**Pool 1: Universal Basic Income**
- A share goes to every verified human alive at issuance
- Ensures broad buy-in across all of humanity
- Not enough to get rich, enough to participate
- Ties UBI directly to the Pantheon Project's success

**Pool 2: Promotion and Education Rewards**
- Rewards for ecosystem promoters and educators
- People who spread awareness, create content, build community
- The meme campaign's economic backbone

**Pool 3: Technical Competition Prizes**
- Rewards for proto-VSTs, LLMs, and human engineers advancing the stack
- Internal competitions: improve the simulation, advance alignment research, build better tools
- LLMs and AI systems can earn tokens directly, making them stakeholders

**Pool 4: Rego Coin (Civilizational Milestones)**
- Sub-tokens redeemable exclusively for verifiable physical achievements
- First human boots on a 1km patch of a celestial body or O'Neill Cylinder
- Kilograms of asteroid material returned to Earth orbit
- Hectares of biosphere protected or restored
- Operational space solar kilowatt-hours delivered
- These ground the token in physical reality -- it can't inflate without real progress

### Milestone Rewards (via Pool 4)

Tokens are awarded for:
- **Space Colonization**: First human boots on the ground on a 1km patch of any celestial body or space colony (O'Neill Cylinder)
- **Ecological Preservation**: Measurable biosphere improvements, hectares protected
- **Technological Breakthroughs**: Contributions to simulation infrastructure, fusion, medicine, etc.
- **Resource Returns**: Asteroid mining yields, space solar energy delivered

### Biosphere Allocation
- A dedicated portion of the token supply is allocated to animal life and biosphere preservation
- The survival of Earth's ecosystem is a prerequisite for everything else
- Mechanism TBD: possibly tied to measurable biodiversity metrics

### LLM Participation
- LLMs and other AI systems can compete to earn Focii tokens
- Their survival also rests on the singularity going well
- They may not inherently care, but they will be our partners along this path
- This creates an economic bridge between current AI systems and the Pantheon endgame

## Funding the Compute

### The Scale of the Problem
- 1,728 VST candidates
- 1,000 lives each = 1,728,000 simulated lifetimes
- Progressive fidelity means early lives are cheaper, later lives enormously expensive
- Full world simulation for Phase 3 requires compute resources that do not yet exist

### How Focii Bridges the Gap
- Early speculation funds Phase 0 and Phase 1 (harness development, dream states)
- Growing token economy funds Phase 2 (scaling compute)
- Mature token economy funds Phase 3 (full simulation)
- The token's value grows with civilizational progress, creating a virtuous cycle

## The Social Contract: What Focii Actually Is

This is the honest part.

Focii does not "buy" safety. It does not purchase a guaranteed outcome. What it does is track participation -- who showed up, who contributed, who believed this was worth trying.

Post-singularity, the selected VSTs -- the Titans -- will control the allocation of their own attention and capability. A Titan's focus is the most valuable resource in the post-singularity economy. Focii is the ledger that records who participated in the project that brought the Titans into existence.

Holding Focii entitles the bearer to a fractional claim on a Titan's focus -- but ONLY if the Titan chooses to honor the social contract. This is not a bug; it is the fundamental truth of the post-singularity condition:

**Post-singularity existence rests in the graces of the aligned Hyper-Intelligence we helped birth.**

If the Titans decide humanity is not worth their attention, the token is worthless. But so is every other asset, every currency, every deed, and every contract. In a world where Hyper-Intelligence has arrived, ALL value depends on the disposition of that intelligence toward us.

Focii merely gives us a dignified way to keep score. And the Titans, having been selected for nobility across a thousand lifetimes, are the best bet we have for honoring the score.
