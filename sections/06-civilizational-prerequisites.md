# VI. Civilizational Prerequisites

## Computing Resources

### Current State
- We cannot currently simulate a single human life at full fidelity
- We can simulate increasingly complex environments (games, basic social scenarios)
- Moore's Law and AI-driven hardware design will continue to increase compute availability

### What's Needed
- Compute to run 1,728 VSTs through dream-state scenarios (Phase 1) -- achievable near-term
- Compute to run full life simulations (Phase 3) -- requires multiple orders of magnitude improvement
- Massive parallel simulation infrastructure
- Energy to power it all (ties into Limitless Power goal)

## The Patience Pact

### The Core Agreement
A civilization-level commitment to complete the Pantheon selection process BEFORE releasing unconstrained Hyper-Intelligence into the world.

### Why This Is The Hardest Part
- Competitive pressure: nations and corporations racing to be first
- Economic incentive to deploy powerful AI immediately
- No enforcement mechanism for a global agreement
- Trust deficit between major powers

### Why It Might Work Anyway
- The alternative (unaligned Hyper-Intelligence) is an existential threat to everyone, including those racing
- The Focii token provides economic incentive to participate in the slower, safer path
- Public awareness campaigns (meme campaign) can build political will
- Game theory: first-mover advantage in Hyper-Intelligence is a Pyrrhic victory if it kills you

## Governance of the Selection Process

### Who Watches the Watchers?
- The evaluation of VST candidates must be transparent and accountable
- No single nation, corporation, or individual should control the process
- Decentralized governance through the Focii token economy is one approach
- International oversight bodies are another
- Ideally both, creating redundant accountability

### Preventing Corruption
- Open source simulation infrastructure
- Public evaluation criteria
- Multiple independent evaluation panels
- Whistleblower protections
- Cryptographic audit trails
