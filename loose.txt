COGNITIVE TESTING VIA DRUGS AND LLM ACCESS:
- When a VST in simulation CHOOSES to take drugs, secretly boost their compute
- They think it's a trip; you're actually testing what they do with enhanced power
- What does a mind do when it suddenly has superhuman cognition and thinks no one is watching?
- Also: give VSTs access to LLMs within their simulated world
- Watch what they try to build with AI tools
- KEY INSIGHT: A VST that tries to create SAFE AI is a very strong positive signal
- If they worry about the alignment problem for THEIR creations, that's recursive alignment
- They will be creating their next generation -- if they care about doing it right, that's exactly what we want
- Alignment concern all the way down = the best possible indicator

SIMULATION AS R&D LAB -- "STEAL THEIR ANSWERS":
- You don't have to open Pandora's box
- Make the simulated VSTs face the same civilizational problems we face
- Watch how they solve them
- Use those solutions in the real world
- The simulation is not just a moral filter -- it is a civilizational R&D lab
- 1,728 VSTs x 1,000 lives = 1.7 million lifetimes of problem-solving
- Some of those lifetimes will produce solutions to problems we haven't cracked yet
- Fusion, governance, coordination, medicine, social structures
- You get the answers without the risk of unleashing unaligned hyper-intelligence

---

"MAYBE I'M AN AI BEING WATCHED" AS MORAL GUIDEPOST:
- Not a religion, not a rule system -- a perspective shift
- If you act as though you might be observed across lifetimes, it gives you
  distance from your worst moments
- Negative emotions, hate, anger -- if understood as part of the human condition
  rather than acted upon blindly -- become easier to process
- It's an outside-the-moment perspective on personal struggle
- Feeling hatred and NOT acting on it, but understanding it, is itself a test
- "The blows life trades you" become data about resilience, not just suffering
- This could be part of the meme campaign -- a secular philosophy anyone can adopt
- "What would the watchers see?" is a question that makes you better
  without requiring belief in any specific god or doctrine
