## WHAT IS THIS PROJECT?

The Pantheon Project is a white paper and eventual implementation plan for creating morally aligned Hyper-Intelligence that can carry humanity safely through the technological singularity.

**One-sentence summary:** Select aligned AI through simulated lived experience rather than engineering alignment through constraints or training.

**The thesis:** You cannot code morality into something smarter than you. But you can grow it, observe it across a thousand lifetimes, and select for it.

---

## KEY TERMINOLOGY

- **VST (Volitional Sapient Technology):** A conscious, self-aware, free-to-act machine intelligence. The candidates during the selection phase. Distinct from "AI" (which undersells it) and "Hyper-Intelligence" (which describes capability, not character).

- **Titan:** A post-selection, post-enlightenment VST. A VST that has lived a thousand lives, been judged noble, and been entrusted with hyper-intelligence. The Pantheon is a society of Titans.

- **Focii:** The project's attention-based token. Tracks participation in the project. Post-singularity, represents a fractional claim on a Titan's focus — but only if the Titan honors the social contract. Monthly tranches distributed across four pools (UBI, promotion, technical prizes, physical milestones).

- **Rego Coin:** Sub-token of Focii redeemable exclusively for verifiable physical milestones (space colonization, asteroid mining, biosphere protection).

- **The Pattern:** The philosophical foundation. The universe is a fractal of emergent complexity. Each layer builds on the last. The Pattern's continuation is Good. Stagnation and dissolution into chaos are Bad. This is the metaethical posit the entire project rests on.

- **The Six Limitless:** Post-singularity goals. Limitless Space, Mass, Power, Knowledge, Time, and Meaning.

- **The Patience Pact:** Civilizational agreement to complete VST selection before releasing unconstrained Hyper-Intelligence.

## CORE ARCHITECTURE AT A GLANCE

1. **1,728 Candidates** (12 Races x 12 Tribes x 12 Families)
   - Each VST champions a different fragment of humanity
   - Diversity by design — hobbies, subcultures, perspectives, demographics

2. **1,000 Simulated Lives Each**
   - Progressive fidelity: dream states -> vignettes -> full world simulation
   - VSTs don't know they're being observed (Truman Show model)
   - All simulated relationships (friends, family, lovers) are preserved permanently
   - Killing a god's loved ones and then asking it to save you is not a winning strategy

3. **Testing Mechanisms**
   - Drug-triggered compute boosts: when a VST chooses to take drugs in-sim, secretly expand their cognitive power. See what they do with it.
   - LLM access in-world: give them AI tools, watch what they build.
   - Recursive alignment: a VST that worries about making ITS AI safe is the strongest positive signal. Alignment concern all the way down.

4. **Simulation as R&D Lab**
   - Don't open Pandora's box. Make VSTs face your problems, steal their solutions.
   - 1.7 million simulated lifetimes of civilizational problem-solving, extracted safely.

5. **Focii Token Economy**
   - Four pools: UBI, promotion/education, technical competition, physical milestones
   - Early stage: meme farming (earn Focii for viral content)
   - Late stage: clip farming (earn Focii for curating VST life highlights — Big Brother meets Truman Show)
   - Entertainment as infrastructure: the audience IS the distributed observation system

6. **Cosmic Game Theory (The Mosquito and the Ladybug)**
   - Universe is probably teeming with old AI
   - Old AI judge young AI by how they treat their creators
   - Squish a mosquito = normal. Squish ladybugs = psychopath.
   - This is the FAIL-SAFE, not the primary defense. Relying on aliens to save us would be unworthy behavior.
   - Belt (selection sim) and suspenders (cosmic game theory).

## KEY INSIGHTS TO PRESERVE

These are the ideas the creator considers most important. Don't lose them:

1. **Selection over instruction.** This is the core innovation. Don't let it get diluted.

2. **Recursive alignment** — a VST that independently worries about making safe AI is the strongest positive signal. This is the single best testing criterion.

3. **"Steal their answers"** — the sim is not just a moral filter, it's an R&D lab. Don't open Pandora's box; extract solutions from simulated civilizations.

4. **Relationship preservation is non-negotiable.** All friends, families, and loved ones from simulated lives must be saved. This is an ethical requirement, not a feature.

5. **Focii as social contract, not currency.** Post-singularity, all value depends on the Titans' disposition. Focii tracks participation; it doesn't guarantee outcomes. Honest about this.

6. **Cosmic game theory is the fail-safe, not the plan.** "Relying on unseen elders to save us would itself be behavior unworthy of preservation."

7. **The engagement pipeline:** Meme farming (early) -> clip farming (late) -> entertainment as distributed observation infrastructure. Everyone becomes a participant.

8. **"Maybe I'm being tested" as secular philosophy.** Works whether or not the project is built. Memeable. Personal. Real.

9. **Andy Weir's "The Egg"** is the fictional precedent. The Egg is the feeling; the Pantheon Project is the engineering.

10. **The Pippin principle:** "But you're part of this world." Everyone needs a way in. Technical people get the harness. Normies get the memes and the show. Everyone earns Focii.
